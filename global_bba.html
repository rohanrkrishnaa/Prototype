The most common concerns — answered honestly.

What if my child is not ready to move abroad after 2 years?+
The programme is designed to be flexible. Students can choose to continue and complete the Global BBA at the India campus without transitioning internationally. There is no forced move — the decision is entirely theirs.
How does the 6-partner destination choice work?+
At the end of Year 2, the student chooses their destination from 6 partner universities across France, USA, and Switzerland. The team provides full guidance — comparing each partner, career outcomes, visa process, and cost — to help make an informed decision.
How safe is it for students to live abroad?+
Students are placed in structured university environments with dedicated international student support, housing guidance, and onboarding systems. Students are fully guided through accommodation and local adaptation before departure — no one navigates this alone.
Will my child adapt to an international academic system?+
Yes. The first 2 years in India are specifically designed to prepare students for international academics — including case-based learning, presentations, and global coursework. This ensures a smooth transition in Year 3.
How does this compare to BBA in India + Masters abroad?+
This pathway gives earlier global exposure (during UG, not after), a stronger international profile for Masters applications, and better career clarity. It reduces the risk of making a high-cost decision after graduation with no prior international experience.
What kind of students succeed in this programme?+
Students who are open to global exposure, comfortable with practical application-based learning, and interested in building careers beyond India. It is less about toppers and more about adaptability, curiosity, and initiative.
Can my child build a career in India after studying abroad?+
Yes. The programme is designed for global as well as India-based careers. With internships, industry exposure, and transferable skills, graduates can pursue opportunities in India, Europe, USA, or other international markets.
